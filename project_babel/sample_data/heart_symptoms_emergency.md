title: Heart Attack Symptoms and When to Seek Emergency Care
category: emergency
---

A heart attack is a medical emergency. Call 9-1-1 immediately if you or someone else has symptoms of a heart attack.

Symptoms of a heart attack may include:

- Chest pain or pressure, or a strange feeling in the chest
- Pain, tingling, or discomfort in one or both arms, back, neck, jaw, or stomach
- Shortness of breath
- Sweating
- Nausea or vomiting
- Lightheadedness or dizziness
- Unusual fatigue

Women may experience different symptoms than men, including:
- Unusual tiredness
- Nausea
- Back or jaw pain
- Shortness of breath without chest pain

Do NOT drive yourself to the hospital if you think you are having a heart attack. Call 9-1-1 and wait for emergency services.

For high blood pressure emergencies, call 9-1-1 if you have:
- Blood pressure over 180/110 with symptoms
- Severe headache with very high blood pressure
- Chest pain or shortness of breath with high blood pressure
- Vision changes with high blood pressure
- Symptoms suggesting a stroke

For non-emergency heart-related concerns such as:
- Questions about blood pressure management
- Medication refills for heart conditions
- General questions about heart health

Contact your family doctor, visit a walk-in clinic, or call 8-1-1 for advice.
