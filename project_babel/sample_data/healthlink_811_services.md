HealthLink BC provides free, non-emergency health information and advice in British Columbia.

Call 8-1-1 (toll-free) to speak with a health service navigator who can help you find health information and services, or connect you directly with a health professional such as:

- Registered nurses available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
- Registered dietitians for nutrition advice
- Pharmacists for medication questions
- Qualified exercise professionals for physical activity guidance

For the deaf and hard of hearing, call 7-1-1.

Translation services are available in over 130 languages on request.

Call 8-1-1 when:
- You are unsure if a health concern is an emergency
- You have health questions but are not sure where to go
- You need help finding health services in your area
- You want advice about symptoms before deciding where to seek care
- You need information about medications, nutrition, or healthy living
- You want to talk to a nurse about a health concern

8-1-1 is NOT for emergencies. For life-threatening conditions, call 9-1-1 or go to your nearest emergency department immediately.

If you do not have a family doctor or nurse practitioner, you can register on the Health Connect Registry to be matched with a primary care provider in your community.
