Walk-in clinics provide access to non-urgent medical care for people who need health advice, assessment and treatment for minor illness and injuries.

Walk-in clinics deal with various health problems and are often open in the evenings and weekends. Usually, you do not need an appointment or you can get an appointment that same day.

Walk-in clinics are appropriate for non-urgent conditions such as:

- Coughs, colds, and sore throat
- Ear infections
- Prescription refills
- Minor skin conditions and rashes
- General health advice
- Follow-up care for minor issues
- Non-urgent medical questions

Walk-in clinics provide treatment services for people who have minor illnesses or injuries that do not require a visit to a hospital emergency department or an urgent care facility.

These programs are generally operated by groups of physicians who are available for patients that do not have family physicians or who need medical treatment when their family physician is not available.

If you need urgent, same day care for conditions that should be seen within 12 to 24 hours, consider an Urgent and Primary Care Centre (UPCC) instead.

If you are visiting a walk-in clinic, ask the doctor if they would be willing to take you on as a patient.
