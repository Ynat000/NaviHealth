##  Need health advice? 

**Call toll-free in B.C.,** [ **[ 8-1-1 ](tel:811) ** ](tel:811) **, or** [ **[ 7-1-1 ](tel:711) ** ](tel:711) **for the deaf and hard of hearing to get personalized assistance. Speak to a navigator who can guide you to reliable health information or connect you with a health professional. Translation services are available in over 130 languages.**
