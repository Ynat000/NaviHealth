##  Overview 

Physical activity refers to any bodily movement produced by skeletal muscles that requires energy expenditure. Examples of physical activity include crawling, walking and cycling. Being physically active has many benefits for physical health, mental health and social well-being. Visit the sections below to learn how physical activity can benefit generally healthy people of all ages and people living with chronic conditions or disabilities and how to take steps toward changing your level of physical activity.
